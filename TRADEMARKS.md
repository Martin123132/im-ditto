# Branding and trademarks

The software licences grant rights to covered software code. They do not grant a right to present a fork, modified version, app, service or store as an official I’m-Ditto, I’m-Store, PC Bridge or Two Hands Network Ltd product. This policy does not assert that any name is a registered trademark.

You may make truthful references such as “an I’m for I’m-Ditto”, “works with I’m-Ditto” or “based on PC Bridge” to describe compatibility or origin. Creators do not need special permission for those descriptions. Make your own publisher identity clear and do not imply official review or endorsement.

Do not use the product or company names, logos or other branding in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement or official status without permission. A licence to starter code is not a general logo licence. Ask Glyn at glyn@twohandsnetwork.co.uk about other branding uses.

Forks should use their own product name and branding unless separately authorised.
