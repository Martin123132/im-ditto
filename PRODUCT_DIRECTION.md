# I’m-Ditto → I’m-Store

## The product — creator first

I’m-Ditto is a common local foundation for making an AI workspace of your own. An I’m is a job-shaped workspace: its interface, tools, saved projects and AI instructions. The human and their connected AI operate the same project. A creator changes the workspace, not the transport every time. The landing page must teach and start this process, not present the bundled examples as the product's main destinations.

There are three examples: the owner's personal PC Bridge is I’m #1, the music-video studio is #2, and the board-game workshop is #3. The common host currently packages the latter two; the personal Bridge remains a separate, preserved application and connection. They demonstrate creating a shell around a job, not a claim that every possible application is supported automatically.

## The foundation delivered in this preview

One local launcher and catalogue; exact-version package review; dependency checks; install, start, stop, update, rollback and data-preserving removal; immutable package identities; separate mutable project data; finite discoverable AI commands; durable local receipts; a working creator starter. Existing PC Bridge is the connection, preserved independently.

## Creator journey

Describe the job on Make my I’m, prepare a separate starter and prompt, ask the connected AI to build within that folder, test the human and command paths, package it, and install the exact reviewed version. How to Ditto teaches the method and presents the examples as build stories and adaptable briefs. My I’ms contains installed workspaces only. No example installation is a prerequisite for creating your own.

Show a real output and the build record alongside each completed example. Reconstructed briefs are labelled as such; milestone evidence is not continuous footage. An I’m should be useful without its creator running a private setup ritual for each user.

## Store direction — not implemented yet

Creators could eventually publish free or paid I’ms. The store could handle discovery, distribution, updates and creator revenue, with Two Hands Network taking a disclosed transaction share. No fee, commercial terms, payment processing, accounts or revenue projections are committed here.

Making your own and choosing a ready-made I’m are complementary. Someone may pay for convenience, thoughtful design, useful specialisation, documented tests, maintenance or support. Those qualities must be demonstrated for each offering, not assumed from its presence in a store. The immediate product is the creation foundation; store demand and commercial success are hypotheses to test later.

Do not turn the local catalogue into an unreviewed executable marketplace. Before third-party distribution: publisher identity and signing, revocation, a defined execution/access policy, supply-chain review, safe update and migration rules, recovery and support, clean-machine installation, privacy controls, creator licensing and payment/refund policies need real implementation and review. Native permission labels currently describe intent; they do not restrict an app at the operating-system level.

## Preview 8 release boundary

Creation is the default landing page, with editable starting briefs and optional examples. Connection checks and installed apps are secondary routes, not prerequisites imposed on a new creator. The independent-user trial has produced an actual user-built I’m; its private project is not distributed or used as marketing material.

The corrected launcher and optional Bridge reply-clock display are implemented. Accessibility remains a separate project, not a release dependency. Missing Bridge clocks do not block creation or ordinary local use.

Preview 8 adds creator licensing, notice preservation and support information. The public preview uses a separate clean release history and a new Windows archive; older development history and release bytes remain private and intact. The owner authorized this preview’s public launch on 7 October 2026. The next product feedback should come from people making their own workspaces, not from expanding the built-in catalogue.

I’m-Ditto and I’m-Store remain the chosen working brand. This is not trademark clearance or a claim of exclusivity over “Ditto.” Store features remain future work.
